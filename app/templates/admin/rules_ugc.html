Rules & UGC

For each department, choose how its UG programmes are filed. UGC: 3-year, Honours and Honours with Research, checked against UGC Table 2. No UGC: semesters 1 to 8 in one flow, no Honours or Research, no Minor, no credit rule — course categories CC, PCC, BSC, ESC, HSMC, DSE, PEC, OE, OEC, GEC, AEC, SEC, VAC, Internship, Project and Dissertation; the tables total whatever the department enters. Design, Allied Healthcare, Engineering and Law are No UGC automatically. PG programmes are not affected.
