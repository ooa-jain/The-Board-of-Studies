Before you begin

A few ground rules for your department's Board of Studies record. Read them once, tick the box, and the portal opens.

{# the one that matters most, set apart #}

Keep your own backup — always

The portal is where your record is submitted; it is not where your department's files are kept. Hold a complete copy of everything you enter and upload — the signed DIAC and DPAC forms, the Pre-BoS and BoS minutes, attendance and photos, each curriculum, syllabus and course revision — in your department's own folder or drive, before and after you submit. If a file is ever questioned, it is your copy that answers.

  1. One login, one department. This username belongs to your department. Share it only with the staff preparing the record, never outside it. Each change is saved under the name and e-mail given when signing in.
  2. What you enter is the official record. Every figure, course and document should match what your Board of Studies approved and the University's own records — nothing estimated, nothing borrowed from another department.
  3. Approved before it goes. The Head of the Department and the Dean approve the record, by name, before the final submit. Their approval is printed in the documents.
  4. Submitted means locked. Once a stage is submitted it cannot be changed from your side; only the Office of Academic Affairs can send it back for correction.
  5. Upload only what belongs. Add the documents each box asks for, and no personal details beyond what the forms require. Uploads are read automatically to check they are the right documents.
  6. How it is used. The record is used by the Office of Academic Affairs for the University's academic records, approvals and accreditation, and is not shared outside the University for any other purpose.
  7. Stuck? Use Query in the menu to watch how a step is done, or Report a bug with a screenshot — the Office will see it.
