Signing in

Every department has one login, issued by the Office of Academics. The username is the department, its school and a number — cse.scse.4821 — so it names a department and never a person.

A department that teaches at more than one campus has one login per campus, because each campus files its own record.

What is checked

  • Credit totals against UGC Table 2, as you type
  • 120 credits for a three-year UG degree, 160 for four-year Honours
  • The in-lieu rule where there is no research project
  • Every required field, before a stage can be submitted
