Behind the scenes

How a submission moves

Each stage unlocks the next. Nothing is accepted until it passes validation, so a department never finds out weeks later that a credit total was short.

The Office of Academics issues a login

One account per department, named after the department code. The Office generates the password and hands it over.

Pre-BoS is filled first

The three signed composition documents — DIAC, Board of Studies and PAC. Everything else stays locked until they are uploaded.

UGC Mandatory Disclosure opens

Programme information, credit distribution and syllabus. The credit table is checked against UGC Table 2 as it is typed.

Evidence, approval, repository

Feedback, meeting papers and the approval chain, ending in a sealed consolidated record for the academic year.

The checking

The credit rules live in the portal, not in a reviewer's head

120

credits is the floor for a three-year UG degree. The portal holds UGC Table 2 and applies the right column for the programme in front of it.

160

for a four-year Honours degree, with the in-lieu rule applied automatically for students who do not take research.

1:25

one credit to twenty-five marks. Marks are checked against credits, contact hours against credits, and category totals against the courses listed.

0

submissions accepted with a total that does not reconcile. A short category is refused at the point of submission, not after.
